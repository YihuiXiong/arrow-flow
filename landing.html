THE DRILLS / 01

Simple cues.
Room to play.

Use a familiar movement, add a clear rule, then set a pace that leaves time to recover.

+01
01 / DIRECTION

Follow the arrow.

A right arrow, a step to your right. Move toward the cue, complete a familiar footwork pattern or shadow stroke, then recover to base.

12345
02 / TARGET

Find your number.

Label cones or floor spots 1–5. See a 3, move to target 3. Or let the color choose the target and the number choose an action.

2 4 4

Same cue. Different response.

Try a number-repeat rule: when the number repeats, stay at base and shadow a stroke instead. You apply the rule; the app supplies the cues.

YOUR SESSION / 02

Set the pace.
Keep it yours.

Build your session
  1. 01

    Choose what you see

    Two, four or eight arrow directions. Your number range and color palette.

  2. 02

    Give each move enough time

    Adjust cue duration and fixed or random waits. Start with a timing preset and make it your own.

  3. 03

    Train. Rest. Go again.

    Set rounds, sets and rest. Add a sound cue or use fullscreen when your screen supports it.

A PRACTICE AID. A CLEAR PURPOSE.

Built for drills.
Honest about the science.

Arrow Flow presents cues for rules you choose. It does not track your movement, measure reaction time or score your response. Benefits to sport performance have not been established for this app.
